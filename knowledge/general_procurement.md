## Separate price from quality
The lowest unit price and the highest quality score are separate conclusions and should be reported separately so the buyer can make the trade-off.
CatalogIQ supplies decision support only and never places an order on the buyer's behalf.

## Normalize before comparing
Before comparing vendors, normalize units and pack sizes so every option is priced on the same basis.

## Freight and minimums
Freight charges and minimum order quantities can change which vendor is cheapest for a small buyer, even when unit prices look close.
If freight or minimums are not in the catalog data, the recommendation should say they were not considered.

## Missing data
When a catalog does not contain a product category, the agent should say so and list the categories it does have rather than estimating prices.
