## Indoor versus outdoor rating
Indoor PVC is suitable for protected indoor displays, while weather-resistant PVC is better for outdoor exposure to rain, sun, and humidity.
Lights rated for indoor use only should not be recommended for storefront exteriors, patios, or rooftop displays.

## Total project cost
Compare total project cost by multiplying unit price by the required pack count, not by comparing single-pack prices alone.
When two vendors use different pack sizes, convert to cost per light before deciding which option is cheaper.

## Seasonal buying window
Seasonal retailers usually place holiday lighting orders months before the selling season, so vendor lead time matters as much as unit price.
A slightly more expensive vendor that ships before the display deadline can be the better business choice.

## Quality signals for lights
For commercial string lights, higher quality usually shows up as weather-resistant jacket material, consistent bulb spacing, and fewer failed bulbs per strand.
A quality score should be read alongside the material, because a high score on an indoor product does not make it safe for outdoor use.
