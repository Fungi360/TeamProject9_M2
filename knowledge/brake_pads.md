## Ceramic versus semi-metallic
Ceramic formulations generally prioritize quiet operation and lower dust; semi-metallic formulations prioritize heat tolerance and durability.
Semi-metallic pads can suit towing or heavy-duty vehicles, while ceramic pads suit daily passenger vehicles where customers notice noise and dust.

## Fit and compatibility
Confirm part fit and axle-set compatibility before recommending an order.
A lower price does not matter if the pad does not match the vehicle's make, model, year, and axle.

## Comebacks and warranty
For a repair shop, a cheaper pad that leads to noise complaints or early wear creates unpaid rework, so quality affects total cost.
Shops often weigh warranty terms and comeback rates alongside the unit price.
