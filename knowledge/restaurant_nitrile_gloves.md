## Latex-free material
For food-service gloves, confirm the material is latex-free and suitable for the intended handling task, since latex allergies affect both staff and guests.
Nitrile is the common latex-free choice for restaurant food handling.

## Cost per glove
Compare cost per glove as well as pack price when pack sizes differ.
A 100-glove box and a 200-glove box cannot be compared on box price alone.

## Task fit and thickness
Thicker nitrile gloves resist tearing during raw-meat prep and cleaning, while thinner gloves give better feel for line assembly work.
A kitchen that changes gloves frequently may prefer a lower-cost glove even if its quality score is lower.

## Supply consistency
Restaurants reorder gloves weekly, so a vendor with steady stock and predictable delivery reduces the risk of running out mid-service.
